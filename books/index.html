Skills Beyond Technology - A guide for students, engineers, and the people who teach them

Skills Beyond Technology is a practical and thought-provoking guide for students, engineers, educators, and lifelong learners navigating a world of rapidly changing technology. As new tools, frameworks, programming languages, and AI systems emerge at an unprecedented pace, it is easy to feel overwhelmed or constantly behind. This book encourages readers to look beyond the pressure of chasing every new technology and instead focus on what continues to matter: strong foundations, problem-solving, critical thinking, learning how to learn, adaptability, communication, and meaningful work. Through reflections on technological change, enduring knowledge, effective learning, and career development, the book offers a practical perspective on building capabilities that remain valuable even as individual technologies come and go. Technology will continue to change, but the ability to think, learn, adapt, and grow can last a lifetime.

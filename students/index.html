Open Problem Statements

Research directions currently open in Dr. GC Jana's group.

Cross-subject epileptic seizure detection from EEG

Build deep-learning models that generalise across patients, reducing the need for per-subject calibration in seizure detection.

EEG · Deep Learning

EEG- and EMG-based speech / imagined-speech decoding

Decode intended speech from neural and muscular signals to support silent-speech and assistive communication interfaces.

EEG · EMG

Mental-stress and cognitive-state estimation from EEG

Develop robust ML pipelines for detecting stress and workload from short EEG segments in real-world conditions.

Machine Learning

Explainable models for neuro-disorder screening

Design interpretable architectures (e.g. GNN / transformer hybrids) for ASD and Alzheimer's screening with clinical explainability.

XAI · Healthcare

Quantum machine learning for biomedical signals

Explore QML approaches for feature learning and classification on EEG/EMG data, and benchmark them against classical baselines.

Quantum ML
